How these warnings are madethe system behind the page

A gradient-boosted model trained on thirteen years of CPCB station history forecasts each city's PM₂.₅ one hour ahead from live Copernicus CAMS readings and live weather. A warning is issued when the forecast National AQI exceeds 150. Health advice follows the CPCB AQI bulletin.

Data: Central Pollution Control Board (station history 2010–2023) · Copernicus CAMS via Open-Meteo (live) · Open-Meteo weather. Warnings are next-hour forecasts, not measurements. 23AID302 — Big Data Analytics.
